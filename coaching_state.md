### Story Details
#### S01 — Setting Strategy & Alignment (Canva UG variant)
- Situation: User Growth was a new strategic group at Canva, focused on MAU — one of the company's two most important metrics alongside ARR. I was asked to build the group, establish strong foundations, and deliver results simultaneously.
- Task: Create a clear product strategy anchored in customer data and embed it across the group.
- Action: Three pillars: (1) delightful first experience (acquisition + activation), (2) when they return, come back more often across more doc types (retention → revenue), (3) explore new virality streams. Strategy wasn't just a document — I built a system: review mechanisms, quarterly planning, alignment requests, kickoffs, fortnightly 1:1s, demos, monthly town halls.
- Result: Within six months: 100+ experiments shipped, 4M MAU impact, $6M ARR impact. Leadership impressed by simultaneous build + delivery.
- Earned Secret: Strategy has to scale. In startups, a fast simple strategy with tight loops. At scale, layered strategies + ongoing comms. Either way: customer centricity, structure, repetition, storytelling.
- Deploy for: "How do you set product strategy at scale" / "What's your approach to aligning teams"

#### S02 — Managing Risk & Planning IC (JSD BHAG)

- Action: Broke delivery into phases. Built JPLAT relationship proactively.
