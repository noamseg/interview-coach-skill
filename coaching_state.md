### Story Details
#### S01 — Setting Strategy & Alignment (Canva UG variant)
- Situation: User Growth was a new strategic group at Canva, focused on MAU — one of the company's two most important metrics alongside ARR. I was asked to build the group, establish strong foundations, and deliver results simultaneously.
- Task: Create a clear product strategy anchored in customer data and embed it across the group.
- Action: Three pillars: (1) delightful first experience (acquisition + activation), (2) when they return, come back more often across more doc types (retention → revenue), (3) explore new virality streams. Strategy wasn't just a document — I built a system: review mechanisms, quarterly planning, alignment requests, kickoffs, fortnightly 1:1s, demos, monthly town halls.
- Result: Within six months: 100+ experiments shipped, 4M MAU impact, $6M ARR impact. Leadership impressed by simultaneous build + delivery.
- Earned Secret: Strategy has to scale. In startups, a fast simple strategy with tight loops. At scale, layered strategies + ongoing comms. Either way: customer centricity, structure, repetition, storytelling.
- Deploy for: "How do you set product strategy at scale" / "What's your approach to aligning teams"

#### S02 — Managing Risk & Planning IC (JSD BHAG)
- Situation: IC PM for JSD next-gen (Donut). BHAG: ship brand-new product configuration experience to 100% of new JSD customers within eight months. Major platform dependencies on JPLAT.
- Task: De-risk delivery while maintaining speed. Hit BHAG OKRs without burning out the team.
- Action: Broke delivery into phases. Built JPLAT relationship proactively. When workflow editor slipped, didn't escalate — thanked them for flagging early, had architects pair immediately, offered to lend our engineers (unheard of at Atlassian). Skin-in-the-game built deep trust and got us back on track.
- Result: Shipped on time. JSD became Atlassian's fastest-growing cloud product. $600M+ ARR, 55,000+ customers. Otto Ruettinger: "Working with JSD feels like a dance instead of a wrestle."
- Earned Secret: Risk management is relationship management. The earlier you build trust with dependencies, the earlier you get real signals.
- Deploy for: "High-stakes platform delivery" / "Managing risk as IC PM" / "How do you work with platform teams"

#### SH — Heidi AI Narrative (structured version)
- Situation: Heidi's underlying AI was genuinely strong — real-time documentation, SOAP notes, specialty-specific models. But it wasn't positioned to win enterprise procurement or differentiate against GPT-based competitors.
